Core Objective 04

Collaborating with Government to Make West Bengal Fit & Healthy

Building Public Health Infrastructure & Synergy

True large-scale transformation requires close collaboration between public administration, local panchayats, sports departments, and grassroots non-governmental organizations. Goti E Jibon actively works alongside district magistrates, municipal corporations, and state sports bodies to implement open-air fitness gyms, community marathons, and preventive health screenings across Bengal.

Key Strategic Collaborations:

  • 1. Public Park Fitness Installations: Partnering with municipalities to install and maintain free open gym equipment and walking tracks in public parks.
  • 2. District-Level Health Marathons: Organizing annual "Goti E Jibon Run for Bengal" marathons to ignite mass physical activity and disease awareness.
  • 3. Village & Panchayat Wellness Drives: Mobilizing mobile medical clinics and health counselors to reach remote rural gram panchayats.
  • 4. Youth Sports Policy Inputs: Assisting policymakers in creating robust scouting mechanisms for national athletic games.
