Core Objective 02

Inviting Retired Individuals to Live a Healthy Life & Serve Society

Honoring Wisdom & Active Longevity

Retirement should not be the end of purposeful living—it is the dawn of giving back to the community while staying physically energized and mentally radiant. Goti E Jibon builds supportive spaces where senior citizens, retired teachers, defense personnel, and professionals lead morning wellness routines, share mentorship with the youth, and enjoy active social companionship.

Key Strategic Initiatives:

  • 1. Senior Vitality & Yoga Clubs: Daily low-impact mobility exercises, gentle stretching, and joint health programs designed specifically for seniors.
  • 2. Youth Mentorship Circles: Pairing experienced retirees with young students for career advice, ethical leadership, and reading circles.
  • 3. Free Preventive Health Checkups: Monthly blood pressure, glucose, and cardiac wellness screenings in local community centers.
  • 4. Companionship & Mental Well-being: Social gatherings, cultural evenings, and storytelling to eliminate loneliness among elderly citizens.
