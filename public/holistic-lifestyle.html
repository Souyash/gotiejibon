Core Objective 05

Promoting Fresh Food, Exercise & Meditation

Reclaiming Pure Health, Vitality & Mental Peace

Modern lifestyle disorders such as diabetes, hypertension, chronic stress, and anxiety stem from ultra-processed food and sedentary living. Goti E Jibon advocates a return to natural vitality through three unbreakable pillars: eating pure, unadulterated farm produce; engaging in daily bodyweight exercise; and practicing pranayama (breathwork) and mindfulness meditation.

Key Holistic Programs:

  • 1. Organic Farm-to-Table Nutrition Education: Guiding families on seasonal eating, microgreen cultivation, and eliminating harmful chemical preservatives.
  • 2. Free Daily Community Fitness Sessions: Open-air morning workout circles featuring calisthenics, functional movement, and Surya Namaskar.
  • 3. Guided Meditation & Stress Relief: Free weekly mindfulness sessions in community halls and parks to reduce anxiety and cultivate inner peace.
  • 4. Youth & School Mindfulness Workshops: Teaching concentration, emotional regulation, and screen-time reduction techniques to children and teens.
