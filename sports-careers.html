Core Objective 01

Building Careers in Sports & Fitness While Empowering Parents

Our Vision & Commitment

In many Indian households, sports has traditionally been viewed purely as a hobby rather than a viable life-long career. Goti E Jibon changes this narrative at the grassroots level. We scout talented young athletes across urban and rural West Bengal, connect them with professional coaching and athletic conditioning, and actively organize structured parental counseling workshops.

Key Strategic Programs:

  • 1. Grassroots Talent Scouting: Conducting athletics, football, cricket, and calisthenics trials across 23 districts in Bengal.
  • 2. Parental Career Counseling: Educating parents on sports science, professional coaching certifications, sports medicine careers, and state sports quotas.
  • 3. Youth Sports Scholarships: Providing high-grade sports gear, nutritional sponsorship, and competition travel aid to underprivileged prodigies.
  • 4. Sports Academy Partnerships: Collaborating with national coaches and certified trainers to provide high-performance training.
