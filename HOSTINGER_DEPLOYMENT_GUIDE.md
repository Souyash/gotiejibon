# 🚀 3-Minute Hostinger Deployment Guide for Goti Jibon

I have pre-packaged the entire production build into **`hostinger-public_html.zip`** located right in this project folder.

Follow these 3 simple steps to make it live on your domain:

---

### Step 1: Log in to Hostinger hPanel
1. Go to [https://hpanel.hostinger.com](https://hpanel.hostinger.com) and log in.
2. Go to **Websites** and click **Manage** next to your domain.

---

### Step 2: Open File Manager
1. In the left sidebar, click **Files** → **File Manager** (or click **Access all files of your domain**).
2. Double-click the **`public_html`** folder to enter it.
*(If there is a default `default.php` or parking file inside `public_html`, you can delete it).*

---

### Step 3: Upload & Extract `hostinger-public_html.zip`
1. Click the **Upload** icon (top-right of File Manager) → choose **File**.
2. Select the file:
   📁 **`hostinger-public_html.zip`** (from your `/Users/souyashbiswas/numPY practice/goti ei/` directory).
3. Right-click the uploaded `hostinger-public_html.zip` in Hostinger and click **Extract**.
4. Choose the current directory (`.` or `public_html`).

---

### 🎉 Done!
Visit **`https://yourdomain.com`** in your browser!
- The full mobile-first website, Hero banner, Founder's message, 5 core pillars, video spotlights, interactive modals, and pre-cached dispatches are now live on the world wide web.
